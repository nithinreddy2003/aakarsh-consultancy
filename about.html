About us

A management consultancy built on partnership.

Aakarsh Consultancy Enterprises Limited is a premier management consultancy specialising in manpower recruitment, headquartered in Hyderabad. ISO 9001:2008 certified.

Since 1997 we have recruited executives at every level, across India and for clients around the world. Every mandate is the start of a long-term partnership built on trust, professionalism and service — never a transaction.

Global reach

Clients across Asia Pacific, Africa, Europe and the Americas.
