Our reach

Roles by level.

From shop-floor to boardroom, we recruit across every seniority level. Every mandate is unique — we treat each with equal professionalism and care.

1

LEVEL 1

Entry & Shop Floor

Foundation roles for freshers, trainees, and entry-level professionals. We focus on quality potential and trainability.

  • Production & utilities workers
  • Quality & safety personnel
  • Merchandisers
  • Freshers & trainees
  • Shop floor supervisors
  • Entry-level analysts
2

LEVEL 2

Leads & Professionals

Mid-career professionals with 3–8 years of experience. These roles carry responsibility for mentoring and technical depth.

  • Developers & software engineers
  • Data & business analysts
  • Team leads & module leads
  • QA leads
  • Chemists & process engineers
  • Database & server administrators
3

LEVEL 3

Management & Specialists

Senior specialists and managers with 8–15 years of experience. Strategic thought and delivery leadership are hallmarks of these roles.

  • Plant & delivery managers
  • Technical architects
  • SAP & software consultants
  • Territory/Account/Zonal managers (TSM/ASM/ZSM)
  • Regulatory & R&D heads
  • Programme & project managers
4

LEVEL 4

Leadership & Executives

Executive and C-suite roles requiring 15+ years of experience. Strategy, vision and transformational impact define these mandates.

  • CIO / CTO / Chief Technology Officer
  • CXOs & delivery heads
  • Functional heads (Engineering, R&D, Operations)
  • Plant & site heads
  • Senior programme managers
  • Board-level advisors

How we work
